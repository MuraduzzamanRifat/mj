08   FAQ Common questions

Common questions.

What is an SEO / AEO / GEO specialist?

Someone who ranks pages on Google (SEO), wins placement in AI answer boxes (AEO), and earns citations in generative AI engines (GEO). I apply all three as one strategy, owning the full loop: audit, plan, execute, measure.

Who is Muraduzzaman?

Senior SEO Specialist at JK Lifestyle Ltd, based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. 5+ years of experience in technical, on-page, and off-page SEO, now applying AEO and GEO for visibility in AI search. 5.0 Upwork rating, with a US client retaining me full-time since 2023.

What services do you offer?

SEO, AEO, and GEO. The most common engagements are technical SEO audits and rebuilds, content architecture and internal linking, structured data, local SEO with Google Business Profile, e-commerce SEO through Google Merchant Center, WordPress and Shopify optimization, and AEO and GEO work for visibility in AI answer boxes and generative AI search.

Where are you based?

Dhaka, Bangladesh, working remotely with clients in the US, UK, Germany, France, Turkey, and Sweden (organic reach verified in GA4). Time zone: Asia/Dhaka (GMT+6). I am comfortable overlapping with US and EU working hours.

What is the difference between SEO, AEO, and GEO?

SEO ranks your pages in Google's search results. AEO (answer engine optimization) wins placement in direct answers such as featured snippets and AI answer boxes. GEO (generative engine optimization) earns citations in AI-generated answers. They build on each other: sound technical SEO and clear, structured content are the base for AEO and GEO.

Are you available for hire?

Yes, for select freelance and contract engagements. The best fit is startups under ~$5M ARR, mid-market growth teams, and agencies subcontracting specialist work. Start here.
