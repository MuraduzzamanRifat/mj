About

I replace the three-vendor stack most companies assume they need.

SEO agency, web developer, automation consultant — one hire handles all three. Delivered by a single operator, end-to-end, without handoffs.

The short version

Senior SEO Specialist with 5+ years of experience in technical SEO, organic growth strategy, and website optimization. My work spans on-page and off-page SEO, semantic SEO and topical authority, E-E-A-T, schema markup and structured data, keyword and SERP research, and both global and local SEO, including Google Business Profile and e-commerce through Google Merchant Center.

Today I focus on AI-powered search. SEO ranks the page, AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) wins the answer box, and GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) earns citations in AI-generated answers. I apply all three, and I build the website and workflow automation that makes the results scale.

I am currently a Senior SEO Specialist at JK Lifestyle Ltd in Dhaka, where I was named Top Performer of the Month in July 2025. In parallel, an international client has retained me full-time on Upwork since 2023, with a 5.0 rating.

Most marketers buy tools. I build them.

Why this combination is rare

A senior SEO specialist can diagnose why your organic traffic is flat. A senior engineer can ship the tool that fixes it. Very few people can do both. A typical growth team either (a) hires three people and coordinates them through Jira, or (b) hires one specialist and outsources everything else to agencies, paying a 40% vendor markup and losing weeks to coordination.

I compress that stack into one contract, one point of accountability, and one delivery timeline.

What's already shipped

  • ProWorkSpace — a Chrome extension SaaS with Stripe billing, a marketing site, and an admin panel. Live, with paying users.
  • Forex Lead Finder — a Python engine that harvests five platforms, scores leads with a 15-point classifier, and stores them in a SQLite warehouse, with a Flask UI and a six-hour run loop.
  • AYVA — a Shopify Dawn fork with zero third-party apps, a Lighthouse mobile score of 95, and 50 migrated products.
  • BrandiVibe — a Three.js brand front-end plus an autonomous AI Sales Brain (GPT-4o) that writes daily SEO articles, scans four startup feeds for ICP-fit leads, researches each one in depth, and runs a warm-up-curve cold-email sequence with self-learning A/B tests. No daily input required.
