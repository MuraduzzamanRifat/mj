Privacy Policy

Short version: this site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and tracks nothing. The only personal data it handles is what you choose to send me through the contact form.

Who this is

This website, mjrifat.com, is the personal portfolio of Muraduzzaman (MJ Rifat), an independent SEO / AEO / GEO specialist based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. Questions about this policy or your data: dev@try-n.com.

No cookies, no tracking

This site does not use cookies, analytics, advertising, pixels, session recording, or any third-party tracking. There is no login and no account. You can browse it without your behaviour being recorded by me.

What I collect, and only when you send it

The single place data is collected is the contact form. When you submit it, you send me your name, email address, and your message. That's it. I use it for one purpose: to read and reply to your enquiry. I do not add you to a newsletter, an auto-DM sequence, or a CRM.

Third parties involved in running the site

  • FormSubmit — delivers contact-form submissions to my email inbox. Your form data passes through their service in transit.
  • Google Fonts — serves the typefaces used on the site; your browser requests them, which can expose your IP address to Google as part of a normal font request.
  • GitHub Pages & Fastly (CDN) — host and serve the static files; like any web host they keep standard server access logs (e.g. IP, timestamp, requested file) for security and operations.

I do not control these providers' own practices; each operates under its own privacy terms.

What I never do

I do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. I do not share it except as needed to reply to you or where legally required.

How long I keep it

I keep enquiry emails for as long as they're useful to our correspondence or a resulting engagement, then delete them. You can ask me to delete your data at any time.

Your rights

You can ask me to access, correct, or delete the personal data you've sent me. Email dev@try-n.com and I'll action it promptly.

Children

This site isn't directed at children and I don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above changes with it. Continued use of the site means you accept the current version.

Questions about your data?

I'll answer honestly and quickly.
